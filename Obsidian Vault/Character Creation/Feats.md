# Magic Feats:

### Elemental Adept:
*Prerequisite: The ability to cast at least one spell*
When you gain this feat, choose one of the following damage types: acid, cold, fire,lightning, or thunder. You gain the following benefits pertaining to the element you selected:
- You learn one cantrip of your choice that deals damage of the chosen type. Choose either Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma as your spellcasting ability for this cantrip.
- Increase an ability score you use for spellcasting by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- Spells you cast ignore resistance to damage of the chosen type. In addition, when you roll damage for a spell you cast that deals damage of that type, you can reroll any damage dice that roll a 1, you must use the result of the new roll.
You can select this feat multiple times. Each time you do so, choose a different damage type.

### Mage Slayer:
You have practiced techniques useful in melee combat against spellcasters, gaining the following benefits:
- Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- When a creature within 5 feet of you starts to cast a spell, you can use your reaction to make a melee weapon attack against that creature.
- When you damage a creature that is concentrating on a spell, that creature has disadvantage on the saving throw it makes to maintain its concentration.
- You have advantage on saving throws against spells cast by creatures within 5 feet of you.

### Magic Initiate:
Choose a class: bard, cleric, druid, sorcerer, warlock, or wizard. You learn two cantrips of your choice from that class’s spell list. In addition, choose one 1st-level spell to learn from that same list. Using this feat, you can cast the spell once at its lowest level, and you must finish a long rest before you can cast it in this way again. You can also cast this spell using spell slots you have of the appropriate level.
You may choose your spellcasting ability for these spells from either intelligence, wisdom, or charisma.

### Metamagic Adept:
*Prerequisite: Spellcasting, Sorcery, or Pact Magic feature*
You’ve learned how to exert your will on your spells to alter how they function:
- You learn two Metamagic options of your choice from the sorcerer class. You can use only one Metamagic option on a spell when you cast it, unless the option says otherwise. Whenever you reach a level that grants the Ability Score Improvement feature, you can replace one of these Metamagic options with another one from the sorcerer class.
- You gain a number of sorcery points equal to your proficiency bonus to spend on Metamagic (these points are added to any sorcery points you have from another source). You regain all spent sorcery points when you finish a long rest.

### Quick Witted:
Your razor sharp wit allows you to sneak in a devastating jest at only a moments notice, you gain the following benefits:
- Increase your Intelligence, or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You learn the [Vicious Mockery](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://docs.google.com/document/d/1myeZgjmFNwZ8qaUIuxEZoetnSreB8hNA4X0bELZ0cJE/edit%23bookmark%3Did.iksan924yjx&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1737762088895840&usg=AOvVaw0ajjFuK5qkcYrkW47jawwD) cantrip. Whenever a creature you can see within 60 ft. of you fails an ability check, or rolls a one on their d20 for an attack roll or saving throw you can use your reaction to cast vicious mockery targeting only that creature. Additionally you can cast [Vicious Mockery](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://docs.google.com/document/d/1myeZgjmFNwZ8qaUIuxEZoetnSreB8hNA4X0bELZ0cJE/edit%23bookmark%3Did.iksan924yjx&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1737762088896174&usg=AOvVaw1UlG5_i7MHVNq2VHizQM5j) as a bonus action a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, regaining all expended uses at the end of a long rest. The spells’ spellcasting ability is the ability increased by this feat.

### Rapid Caster:
*Prerequisite: The ability to cast at least one spell of third level or higher*
You have mastered the art of unleashing your spells in rapid succession. You gain the following benefits:
- Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- When you cast a spell as a bonus action on your turn, you are not restricted in the level of spell that you can cast when casting another spell during that turn. You cannot cast the same spell with your action and bonus action on the same turn, and you do not gain this benefit when casting spells with the quickened spell metamagic.
- Select one spell of second level or lower with a casting time of one action that you know. You can cast this spell as a bonus action, still expending the spell slot as usual, once you cast the spell in this way, you cannot do so again until you complete a short or long rest.

### Ritual Caster:
*Prerequisite: Intelligence or Wisdom 13 or higher*
You have learned a number of spells that you can cast as rituals. These spells are written in a ritual book, which you must have in hand while casting one of them.
When you choose this feat, you acquire a ritual book holding two 1st-level spells of your choice. Choose one of the following classes: bard, cleric, druid, sorcerer, warlock, or wizard. You must choose your spells from that class’s spell list, and the spells you choose must have the ritual tag. The class you choose also determines your spellcasting ability for these spells: Charisma for bard, sorcerer, or warlock; Wisdom for cleric or druid; or Intelligence for wizard.
If you come across a spell in written form, such as a magical spell scroll or a wizard’s spellbook, might be able to add it to your ritual book. The spell must be on the spell list for the class you chose, the spell’s level can be no higher than half your level (rounded up), and it must have the ritual tag. The process of copying the spell into your ritual book takes 2 hours per level of the spell, and costs 50 gp per level. The cost represents material components you expend as you experiment with the spell to master it, as well as the fine inks you need to record it.

### Spell Focus:
*Prerequisite: The ability to cast at least one spell of third level or higher, and both a first and second level spell slot*
Your persistent focus and practice allow you to cast your chosen spells with more consistency than other spellcasters:
- Your maximum number of 1st and 2nd level spell slots are each decreased by one.
- Select one first level and one second level spell with a casting time of one action you know and can cast. You can cast each of these spells once without expending a spell slot, and regain the ability to do so after completing a short or long rest.

### Spell Sniper:
*Prerequisite: The ability to cast at least one spell*
You have learned techniques to enhance your attacks with certain kinds of spells, gaining the following benefits:
- When you cast a spell that requires you to make an attack roll, the spell’s range is doubled.
- Your ranged spell attacks ignore half cover and three-quarters cover.
- You learn one cantrip that requires an attack roll. Choose the cantrip from the bard, cleric, druid, sorcerer, warlock, or wizard spell list. Your spellcasting ability for this cantrip depends on the spell list you chose from: Charisma for bard, sorcerer, or warlock; Wisdom for cleric or druid; or Intelligence for wizard.

### Telekinetic:
You learn to move things with your mind, granting you the following benefits:
- Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You learn the mage hand cantrip. You can cast it without verbal or somatic components, and you can make the spectral hand invisible. If you already know this spell, its range increases by 30 feet when you cast it. Its spellcasting ability is the ability increased by this feat.
- As a bonus action, you can try to telekinetically shove one creature you can see within 30 feet of you. When you do so, the target must succeed on a Strength saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + the ability modifier of the score increased by this feat) or be moved 5 feet toward you or away from you. A creature can willingly fail this save.

### Telepathic:
You awaken the ability to mentally connect with others, granting you the following benefits:
- Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You can speak telepathically to any creature you can see within 60 feet of you. Your telepathic utterances are in a language you know, and the creature understands you only if it knows that language. Your communication doesn’t give the creature the ability to respond to you telepathically.
- You can cast the detect thoughts spell, requiring no spell slot or components, and you must finish a long rest before you can cast it this way again. Your spellcasting ability for the spell is the ability increased by this feat. If you have spell slots of 2nd level or higher, you can cast this spell with them.

### War Caster:
*Prerequisite: The ability to cast at least one spell*
You have practiced casting spells in the midst of combat, learning techniques that grant you the following benefits:
- You have advantage on Constitution saving throws that you make to maintain your concentration on a spell when you take damage.
- You can perform the somatic components of spells even when you have weapons or a shield in one or both hands.
- When a hostile creature’s movement provokes an opportunity attack from you, you can use your reaction to cast a spell at the creature, rather than making an opportunity attack. The spell must have a casting time of 1 action and must target only that creature.

## Planetouched Feats:
### Feywild:
Your exposure to the Feywild’s magic has changed you, granting you the following benefits:
- Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You learn the misty step spell and one 1st-level spell of your choice. The spell must be one of Faerie Fire, Color Spray, Animal Friendship, Command, Disguise Self, Hideous Laughter, Silent Image, Sleep, or Charm Person. You can cast each of these spells without expending a spell slot. Once you cast either of these spells in this way, you can’t cast that spell in this way again until you finish a long rest. You can also cast these spells using spell slots you have of the appropriate level. The spells’ spellcasting ability is the ability increased by this feat.

### Shadowfell:
Your exposure to the Shadowfell’s magic has changed you, granting you the following benefits:
- Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You learn the invisibility spell and one 1st-level spell of your choice. The spell must be one of Hex, False life, Bane, Cause Fear, Ray of Sickness, Inflict Wounds, or Disguise self. You can cast each of these spells without expending a spell slot. Once you cast either of these spells in this way, you can’t cast that spell in this way again until you finish a long rest. You can also cast these spells using spell slots you have of the appropriate level. The spells’ spellcasting ability is the ability increased by this feat.

### Far Realm:
Your exposure to the Far Realm’s magic has changed you, granting you the following benefits:
- Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You learn the [mind whip](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://docs.google.com/document/d/19T-fhbu4amcqYw4xRej_Qkaf9xUnuzjrYeiKILdduP4/edit%23bookmark%3Did.wgso5l19bpkq&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1737762088902078&usg=AOvVaw10FfVN3v7mymu9QkmL1wrZ) spell and one 1st-level spell of your choice. The spell must be one of Arms of Hadar, Bane, Comprehend Languages, Dissonant Whispers, Hideous Laughter, Magnify Gravity, or Disguise self. You can cast each of these spells without expending a spell slot. Once you cast either of these spells in this way, you can’t cast that spell in this way again until you finish a long rest. You can also cast these spells using spell slots you have of the appropriate level. The spells’ spellcasting ability is the ability increased by this feat.

### Abyss:
Your exposure to the Abyss’s magic has changed you, granting you the following benefits:
- Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You learn the alter creature spell and one 1st-level spell of your choice. The spell must be one of Chaos Bolt, Inflict Wounds, Ray of Sickness, Bad Blood, Absorb Elements, Hex, Summon Ooze, or Protection From Evil and Good. You can cast each of these spells without expending a spell slot. If you cast alter self in this way, it can only target yourself, and it does not require concentration. Once you cast either of these spells in this way, you can’t cast that spell in this way again until you finish a long rest. You can also cast these spells using spell slots you have of the appropriate level. The spells’ spellcasting ability is the ability increased by this feat.

### Celestia:
Your exposure to mount celestia’s magic has changed you, granting you the following benefits:
- Increase your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You learn the prayer of healing spell and one 1st-level spell of your choice. The spell must be one of Bless, Guiding Bolt, Purify Food and Drink, Sanctuary, Shield of Faith, Cure Wounds, or Command. You can cast each of these spells without expending a spell slot. Once you cast either of these spells in this way, you can’t cast that spell in this way again until you finish a long rest. You can also cast these spells using spell slots you have of the appropriate level. The spells’ spellcasting ability is the ability increased by this feat.

# Martial Feats:
## Armor Feats:
### Lightly Armor Training:
You have trained to master the use of light armor, gaining the following benefits:
- Increase your Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You gain proficiency with light armor.

### Light Armor Master:
*Prerequisite: Proficiency with light armor*
You can use your armor to deflect strikes that would kill others. You gain the following benefits:
- Increase your Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- Opportunity attacks from moving are made against you with Disadvantage.
- The first attack made against you in between each of your turns is made with disadvantage.

### Medium Armor Training:
*Prerequisite: Proficiency with light armor*
You have trained to master the use of medium armor and shields, gaining the following benefits:
- Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You gain proficiency with medium armor.

### Medium Armor Master:
*Prerequisite: Proficiency with medium armor*
You have practiced moving in medium armor to gain the following benefits:
- Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- Wearing medium armor doesn’t impose disadvantage on your Dexterity (Stealth) checks.
- While wearing medium armor your AC is increased by 1.

### Heavy Armor Training:
*Prerequisite: Proficiency with medium armor*
You have trained to master the use of heavy armor, gaining the following benefits:
- Increase your Strength score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You gain proficiency with heavy armor.

### Heavy Armor Master:
*Prerequisite: Proficiency with heavy armor*
You can use your armor to deflect strikes that would kill others. You gain the following benefits:
- Increase your Strength or Constitution score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- While you are wearing heavy armor, bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage that you take from non-magical attacks is reduced by an amount equal to your proficiency bonus.

## Damage Feats:
### Crusher:
You are practiced in the art of crushing your enemies, granting you the following benefits:
- Increase your Strength or Constitution by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- Once per turn, when you hit a creature with an attack that deals bludgeoning damage, you can move it 5 feet to an unoccupied space, provided the target is no more than one size larger than you.
- When you score a critical hit that deals bludgeoning damage to a creature, attack rolls against that creature are made with advantage until the start of your next turn.

### Piercer:
You have achieved a penetrating precision in combat, granting you the following benefits:
- Increase your Strength or Dexterity by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- Once per turn, when you hit a creature with an attack that deals piercing damage, you can deal additional damage to the target equal to your strength or dexterity modifier (your choice).
- When you score a critical hit that deals piercing damage to a creature, you can roll one additional damage die when determining the extra piercing damage the target takes.

### Slasher:
You’ve learned where to cut to have the greatest results, granting you the following benefits:
- Increase your Strength or Dexterity by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- Once per turn when you hit a creature with an attack that deals slashing damage, you can reduce the speed of the target by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.
- When you score a critical hit that deals slashing damage to a creature, you grievously wound it. Until the start of your next turn, the target has disadvantage on all attack rolls.

## Mastery Feats:

### Axe Master:
*Prerequisite: Level 8 or higher*
Your strength allows you to wield axes to deliver devastating strikes:
- [Heavy attacks](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://docs.google.com/document/d/1r_OR3zTVmMnoeD4KjLYYMeLsSP5FPb2F0FYGNc2MIek/edit%23bookmark%3Did.4w1j0bqx9agd&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1737762088909861&usg=AOvVaw0YXgW0jABuU9M1llxQ12DE) made with axes have their bonus damage increased to +10
- When you attack with an axe and the d20 result is 16 or higher you can roll one additional weapon damage die when determining the damage of the attack.
- On your turn, when you hit a creature that has all its hit points with an attack made with an axe you can make one additional attack against that creature as a bonus action.
- All axes gain the thrown (range 30/60) property.

### Blade Master:
*Prerequisite: Level 8 or higher*
Your skill with bladed weapons allows you to punish even the smallest openings left by your foes:
- [Heavy attacks](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://docs.google.com/document/d/1r_OR3zTVmMnoeD4KjLYYMeLsSP5FPb2F0FYGNc2MIek/edit%23bookmark%3Did.4w1j0bqx9agd&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1737762088910949&usg=AOvVaw32c_J263BONpNax6Dtn_az) made with blades have their bonus damage increased to +10
- When you hit a creature that is suffering from the blinded, dazed, grappled, paralyzed, prone, restrained, stunned, slowed, flanked, or surrounded condition with a weapon attack made with blade you can make one additional attack with a blade as a bonus action.
- You add both your strength and dexterity modifier (minimum 1 each)  to damage rolls for attacks you make with blades.
- When you make a melee weapon attack with a blade as part of a readied action you can still benefit from your extra attack feature.

### Bow master:
*Prerequisite: Level 8 or higher*
Your skill with the bow allows you to make precise attacks that can control the flow of the battlefield or catch eager enemies unawares.
- As a bonus action you can mark a creature until the start of your next turn, if the marked creature willingly moves 5 ft. in any direction you can use your reaction to make a ranged weapon attack against it with a bow or crossbow you are wielding. The marked creature is aware of this effect.
- When you take the disengage action on your turn, you can make a single ranged weapon attack as a bonus action.
- When you roll damage for weapon attack made with a bow or crossbow and roll the maximum possible value on the damage die the attack deals additional damage equal to your proficiency bonus.

### Blunt Weapon Master:
*Prerequisite: Level 8 or higher*
Your incredible skill with bludgeoning weapons allows you to deliver blows strong enough to crush through your opponent’s defenses and leave them disoriented.
- [Heavy attacks](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://docs.google.com/document/d/1r_OR3zTVmMnoeD4KjLYYMeLsSP5FPb2F0FYGNc2MIek/edit%23bookmark%3Did.4w1j0bqx9agd&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1737762088912409&usg=AOvVaw3TrB_g4T3jND7AZFtYdkk7) made with bludgeons have their bonus damage increased to +10
- When you hit a creature with a melee attack made with a bludgeon, that creature has disadvantage on the next attack it makes on its turn.
- When you attack with a bludgeon and miss by 5 or less, the target creature suffers bludgeoning damage equal to 1d4 plus your strength modifier.

### Crossbow Master:
*Prerequisite: Level 8 or higher*
Thanks to extensive practice with the crossbow, you gain the following benefits:
- You ignore the loading property of crossbows with which you are proficient.
- Being within 5 feet of a hostile creature doesn’t impose disadvantage on your ranged attack rolls made with crossbows
- When you use the Attack action and attack with a one-handed weapon, you can use a bonus action to make an [off-hand](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://docs.google.com/document/d/1r_OR3zTVmMnoeD4KjLYYMeLsSP5FPb2F0FYGNc2MIek/edit%23bookmark%3Did.vdr835bxcow&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1737762088913533&usg=AOvVaw2MKSyf-WOO90hPN0sl7S3S) attack with a hand crossbow you are holding.

### Great Weapon Master:
*Prerequisite: Level 8 or higher*
You’ve learned to put the weight of a weapon to your advantage, letting its momentum empower your strikes. You gain the following benefits:
- [Heavy attacks](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://docs.google.com/document/d/1r_OR3zTVmMnoeD4KjLYYMeLsSP5FPb2F0FYGNc2MIek/edit%23bookmark%3Did.4w1j0bqx9agd&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1737762088914017&usg=AOvVaw2pC4IJ9BsWrWT88tlKcBIP) made with heavy melee weapons that lack the reach property have their bonus damage increased to +10
- On your turn, when you score a critical hit with a heavy melee weapon that lacks the reach property or reduce a creature to 0 hit points with one, you can make one melee weapon attack as a bonus action.
- While wielding a heavy melee weapon that lacks the reach property you add twice your strength modifier to your damage rolls in place of the usual amount.

### Polearm Master:
*Prerequisite: Level 8 or higher*
You have learned to leverage the reach and speed of your polarms to make blindingly fast attacks.
- [Heavy attacks](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://docs.google.com/document/d/1r_OR3zTVmMnoeD4KjLYYMeLsSP5FPb2F0FYGNc2MIek/edit%23bookmark%3Did.4w1j0bqx9agd&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1737762088914839&usg=AOvVaw3waISKtfwWyeTWRTBo6mQu) made with polearms have their bonus damage increased to +10
- While you are wielding a polearm other creatures provoke an opportunity attack from you when they enter the reach you have with that weapon, or if they hit you with a melee attack from outside your reach. You must make this attack with the polearm you are wielding.
- As a bonus action on your turn you can brace your polearm to deal a devastating blow against the next creature to enter your reach. Until the start of your next turn, whenever a creature triggers an opportunity attack as a result of entering your reach or hitting you with an attack, the creature takes 1d10 additional damage, this damage increases by 1d10 for each level of size above medium for the target creature.

### Shield Master:
*Prerequisite: Level 8 or higher*
You use shields not just for protection but also for offense. You gain the following benefits while you are wielding a shield:
- While you have a shield equipped, if you hit a creature within 5 feet of you with a weapon attack, you can immediately use a bonus action to try to shove that creature with your shield.
- If you aren’t incapacitated, you can add your shield’s AC bonus to any Dexterity saving throw you make against a spell or other harmful effect that targets you. This benefit also applies to creatures of your choice within 5 feet of you.
- If you are subjected to an effect that allows you to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, you instead take no damage if you succeed on the saving throw, interposing your shield between yourself and the source of the effect.

### Skirmisher:
*Prerequisite: Level 8 or higher*
You've learned how to harass your opponents from afar in combat, leaving them off balance when you close in to strike. You gain the following benefits:
- When you take the attack action and make at least one ranged or thrown weapon attack while not within a hostile creature's reach you can make one melee weapon attack against that creature as a bonus action, as long as the melee weapon you use does not have the reach property.
- When you hit a creature with a ranged or thrown weapon attack, you have advantage on your next melee weapon attack against that creature before the end of your next turn.
- When you take the Disengage action your speed increases by 10ft. for the rest of your turn.

## Misc Martial Feats:

### Defensive Duelist:
*Prerequisite: Dexterity 13 or higher*
In combat you have mastered the art of fighting without leaving a single opening for your opponent. You gain the following benefits:
- Increase your Strength, Dexterity, or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- When a creature within 5 feet of you misses you with an attack, you can use your reaction to grant that creature disadvantage on all subsequent attacks against you until the end of your next turn. If you are wielding a parrying dagger, this reaction is automatically used as part of the reaction described in the [parrying dagger's special trait](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://docs.google.com/document/d/194P4rrzRulii5TKh30wgP8ASH4T3JIWmBy1DjpPXLnc/edit%23bookmark%3Did.hnr5j631cwal&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1737762088916998&usg=AOvVaw2__79B1usSkVNkdU7KMMlL).
- Whenever you hit a creature with a melee attack you can move 5 feet without spending any movement. This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks.

### Dual Wielder:
You master fighting with two weapons, gaining the following benefits:
- You gain a +1 bonus to AC while you are wielding a separate melee weapon in each hand.
- You can make [off-hand](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://docs.google.com/document/d/1r_OR3zTVmMnoeD4KjLYYMeLsSP5FPb2F0FYGNc2MIek/edit%23bookmark%3Did.vdr835bxcow&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1737762088917828&usg=AOvVaw3fcg3ww2F9KdbHrsYPgfa_) attacks even when the one-handed melee weapons you are wielding aren’t light.
- Once per turn when you miss with a weapon attack, you can immediately make an off-hand attack on the same target with a one-handed melee weapon held in the other hand, following the usual rules for [off-hand](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://docs.google.com/document/d/1r_OR3zTVmMnoeD4KjLYYMeLsSP5FPb2F0FYGNc2MIek/edit%23bookmark%3Did.vdr835bxcow&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1737762088918344&usg=AOvVaw3LqmMTOGOW1vi0cd3SArb6) attacks.

### Guardian:
In combat you are able to protect your allies from your enemies attacks, gaining the following benefits:
- Increase your Strength, or Constitution by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- When a creature targets an ally within 5 feet of you with an attack you can use your reaction to grant that creature disadvantage on its attack roll. If the attack still hits, the target has resistance to the damage from that attack. You can only use this reaction if you are wielding a weapon or a shield.

### Martial Adept:
You have martial training that allows you to perform special combat maneuvers. You gain the following benefits:
- You learn two exploits of your choice from among those available to fighter class. If a maneuver you use requires your target to make a saving throw to resist the maneuver’s effects, the saving throw DC equals your superiority save DC, or 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength or Dexterity modifier (your choice) if you do not already have a superiority save DC.
- You gain two superiority die, which are d6s (these dice are added to and scale with any superiority dice you have from another source). These dice are used to fuel your maneuvers. A superiority die is expended when you use it. You regain your expended superiority dice when you finish a short or long rest.

### Sentinel:
You have mastered techniques to take advantage of every drop in any enemy’s guard, gaining the following benefits:
- When you hit a creature with an opportunity attack, the creature’s speed becomes 0 for the rest of the turn.
- Creatures provoke opportunity attacks from you even if they take the Disengage action before leaving your reach.
- When a creature within 5 feet of you makes an attack against a target other than you, you can use your reaction to make an opportunity attack with a melee weapon against the attacking creature.

### Sharpshooter:
You have mastered ranged weapons and can make shots that others find impossible. You gain the following benefits:
- When you make a ranged weapon attack, the maximum distance for the weapon's normal range is doubled. A weapon's maximum normal range can never exceed its long range.
- You can make [heavy attacks](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://docs.google.com/document/d/1r_OR3zTVmMnoeD4KjLYYMeLsSP5FPb2F0FYGNc2MIek/edit%23bookmark%3Did.4w1j0bqx9agd&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1737762088920400&usg=AOvVaw3VeZs4xd0Mi7o6pMA7CZRQ) with any ranged weapon you are wielding.

### Warrior Training:
You have practiced extensively with a variety of weapons, gaining the following benefits:
- You gain proficiency with all simple and martial weapons.
- You gain one Fighting Style from the fighter class that you do not already have.

# Misc Feats:

## Skill Feats:
### Alert:
Always on the lookout for danger, you gain the following benefits:
- You gain a bonus to your initiative rolls equal to your proficiency bonus.
- You can’t be surprised while you are conscious.
- Other creatures don’t gain advantage on attack rolls against you as a result of being unseen by you.
- Immediately after you roll initiative, you can swap your initiative with one willing ally in the same combat. You cannot make this swap if you or the ally is incapacitated.

### Actor:
Skilled at mimicry and dramatics, you gain the following benefits:
- Increase your Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You have advantage on Charisma (Deception) and Charisma (Performance) checks when trying to pass yourself off as a different person.
- You can mimic the speech of another person or the sounds made by other creatures. You must have heard the person speaking, or heard the creature make the sound, for at least 1 minute. A successful Wisdom (Insight) check contested by your Charisma (Deception) check allows a listener to determine that the effect is faked.
- You gain proficiency with Performance, disguise kits and weaver’s tools.

### Athlete:
You have undergone extensive physical training to gain the following benefits:
- Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- When you are prone, standing up uses only 5 feet of your movement.
- Your movement speed increases by 10 feet.
- Climbing and swimming don’t cost you extra movement.
- You can make a running long jump or a running high jump without any previous movement, rather than 10 feet.
- Whenever you Ready an action, when the trigger occurs and you use your reaction, you can also move up to your speed minus any movement used during the turn in which you readed the action.

### Dungeon Delver:
Alert to the hidden traps and secret doors found in many dungeons, you gain the following benefits:
- Increase your Wisdom or Constitution score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You have darkvision out to a range of 60 feet. If you already have darkvision, its range increases by 30 feet.
- Your passive perception and investigation is considered 20 for the purposes of detecting hidden doors.
- You have advantage on saving throws made to avoid or resist traps.
- You have resistance to the damage dealt by traps.
- Traveling at a fast pace doesn't impose the normal −5 penalty on your passive Wisdom (Perception) score.
- You always know which way is north.
- You always know the number of hours left before the next sunrise or sunset.

### Grappler:
*Prerequisite: Strength 13 or higher*
You’ve developed the skills necessary to hold your own in close-quarters grappling. You gain the following benefits:
- Increase your Strength score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You are considered to be one size larger for the purposes of shoving and grappling creatures.
- You have advantage on attack rolls (including shoving) against a creature you are grappling.
- Your speed is no longer halved when moving a grappled creature with you.
- When you make an opportunity attack, you can make a grapple attempt instead of a weapon attack.
- When a creature you are grappling attempts to break your grapple and fails, or attempts to shove you and fails, you can use your reaction to make a melee attack against them.
- When you restrain a creature you have grappled, you are not restrained as well.

### Healer:
You are an able physician, allowing you to mend wounds quickly and get your allies back in the fight. You gain the following benefits:
- When you use a healer’s kit to stabilize a dying creature, that creature instead regains hit points equal to your bonus to the medicine skill
- As an action, you can spend one use of a healer’s kit to tend to a creature and restore 1d6 + your bonus to the medicine skill hit points to it. A creature can only regain hit points in this way once per short or long rest

### Inspiring Leader:
*Prerequisite: Charisma 13 or higher*
You can spend 10 minutes inspiring your companions, shoring up their resolve to fight. When you do so, choose any number of friendly creatures (which can include yourself) within 30 feet of you who can see or hear you and who can understand you. Each creature can gain temporary hit points equal to your level + your Charisma modifier. A creature can’t gain temporary hit points from this feat again until it has finished a short or long rest.

### Linguist:
You have studied languages and codes, gaining the following benefits:
- Increase your Intelligence score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You learn three languages of your choice.
- You can ably create written ciphers. Others can’t decipher a code you create unless you teach them, or they succeed on an Intelligence check (DC equal to your Intelligence score + your proficiency bonus + the number of languages you know), or they use magic to decipher it.

### Observant:
Quick to notice details of your environment, you gain the following benefits:
- Increase your Intelligence or Wisdom score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- If you can see a creature’s mouth while it is speaking a language you understand, you can interpret what it’s saying by reading its lips.
- You have a +5 bonus to your passive Wisdom (Perception) and passive Intelligence (Investigation) scores.

### Prodigy:
You have a knack for learning new things. You gain the following benefits:
- Increase your Intelligence or Wisdom score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You gain one skill proficiency of your choice, one tool proficiency of your choice, and fluency in one language of your choice.
- Choose one tool or skill in which you have proficiency. You gain expertise with that skill.

## Crafting Feats:
### Alchemist:
Time spent experimenting with potions and alchemical extracts has led to greater skill in the use and creation of such items, granting you the following benefits:
- Increase your Intelligence or Wisdom score by 1, up to a maximum of 20
- Potions of healing that you create restore additional hit points equal to half of the potions usual maximum healing value.
- You can drink any potion as a bonus action, and gain the full effects of the potion whenever you do so.
- You gain expertise with the alchemy kit if you do not already have it.
- *Crafting Feat*. You gain access to a greater list of recipes to create potions and alchemical solutions, and are more skilled at finding the materials used to craft them.

### Chef:
Time spent mastering the culinary arts has paid off, granting you the following benefits:
- Increase your Constitution or Wisdom score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You gain expertise with cook’s utensils if you don’t already have it.
- As part of a short rest, you can cook delicious food, provided you have ingredients and cook’s utensils on hand. You can prepare enough of this food for a number of creatures equal to 4 + your proficiency bonus. At the end of the short rest, any creature who eats the food and spends one or more Hit Dice to regain hit points regains extra hit points equal to 1d8 + your proficiency bonus.
- *Crafting Feat*. You gain access to a greater list of recipes to create food items, and are more skilled at finding the materials used to craft them.

### Poisoner:
You are well practiced in the subtle art of using poison to debilitate your foes, granting you the following benefits:
- Increase your Dexterity, Intelligence, or Wisdom score by 1, up to a maximum of 20
- Whenever you use a poison, you may use your Intelligence or Wisdom saving throw DC in place of the save DC of the item if yours is higher.
- Any poisons you apply to your own weapons remain potent for up to two additional hits, however they still lose their potency as soon as a creature fails its saving throw against the poison. Additionally, poisons that still deal half their damage to a creature on a successful saving throw instead deal no damage on a successful saving throw, unless it is the last use of the poison before it loses its potency.
- You can apply any poison or oil to a weapon you are wielding as a bonus action.
- You gain expertise with the poisoners kit if you do not already have it.
- *Crafting Feat*. You gain access to a greater list of recipes to create poisons, and are more skilled at finding the materials used to craft them.

### Scribe:
You are well practiced in the art of creating spell scrolls, granting you the following benefits:
- Increase your Wisdom or Intelligence score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- When you cast a spell using a spell scroll, you may use your own spell save DC instead of the spell save DC of the scroll if yours is higher.
- You can use any spell scroll as if that spell was on your class's spell list and of a spell level you can cast.
- *Crafting Feat*. You gain access to a greater list of recipes to create spell scrolls, and are more skilled at finding the materials used to craft them.

### Tinkerer:
Years of practice working on and creating mechanical contraptions have sharpened your skills as a tinkerer, you gain the following benefits:
- Increase your Dexterity, Intelligence score by 1, up to a maximum of 20
- Whenever you use a bomb or trap, you may use your Dexterity or Intelligence DC in place of the save DC of the item if yours is higher.
- You gain expertise with thieves tools and tinkers tools if you don’t already have it.
- *Crafting Feat*. You gain access to a greater list of recipes to create bombs, traps, and other mechanical contraptions, and are more skilled at finding the materials used to craft them.

## Physical Feats:
### Candle Burning at Both Ends:
Adventurers often disregard grievous injury to continue fighting at the expense of their future well-being. You gain the following benefits.
- Increase your Constitution score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- If you would fall to 0 hit points or die, instead, you may permanently mark a death saving throw as failed and instead your hit points become equal to three times your level. If all death saving throws are marked as failed, any time you are reduced to zero hit points you die and can only be restored to life by means of a true resurrection or a wish spell.

### Charger:
*Prerequisite: Strength 13 or higher*
Your speed and strength allow you to throw yourself into battle with incredible fervor. You gain the following benefits:
- Increase your Strength score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- When you take the Dash action, your Speed increases by 10 feet for that turn. Additionally, you can make a single melee weapon attack as part of the dash action, on a hit you can push the target up to 10 feet away if it is no more than one size larger than you. Starting at 5th level this attack also does an additional 1d8 bludgeoning damage.

### Durable:
*Prerequisite: Constitution 13 or higher*
Hardy and resilient, you gain the following benefits:
- Increase your Constitution score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You double the bonus granted by your constitution score to your hit die rolls made to restore hit points during a short rest.
- When you would gain a level of the [injured](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://docs.google.com/document/d/10HO7-PWr6UebiqzbOMRmuynXUPcoPzRh578z2O_lOHA/edit%23bookmark%3Did.g5pqwxkoofgu&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1737762088932155&usg=AOvVaw1CyPJvX_QDNU3FceTnbhRg) condition, you can choose to instead gain one level of exhaustion.
- You have advantage on death saving throws. If you would ever suffer instant death, you are instead reduced to 0 hit points and suffer 2 death saving throw failures. Critical hits against you while you are DBNO result in a single failed death save, instead of two.

### Mobile:
*Prerequisite: Dexterity 13 or higher*
You are exceptionally speedy and agile. You gain the following benefits:
- Your speed increases by 10 feet.
- When you use the Dash action, difficult terrain doesn’t cost you extra movement on that turn.
- When you make a melee attack against a creature, you don’t provoke opportunity attacks from that creature for the rest of the turn, whether you hit or not.

### Resilient:
Choose one ability score. You gain the following benefits:
- Increase the chosen ability score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You gain proficiency in saving throws using the chosen ability.

### Skulker:
*Prerequisite: Dexterity 13 or higher*
You are expert at slinking through shadows. You gain the following benefits:
- Increase your Dexterity score by 1,to a maximum of 20.
- You can take the Hide action as a bonus action.
- You can try to hide when you are lightly obscured from the creature from which you are hiding.
- When you are hidden from a creature and miss it with a ranged weapon attack, making the attack doesn’t reveal your position.
- Dim light doesn’t impose disadvantage on your Wisdom (Perception) checks relying on sight.

### Tavern Brawler:
Accustomed to rough-and-tumble fighting using whatever weapons happen to be at hand, you gain the following benefits:
- Increase your Strength, Dexterity,  or Constitution score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You are proficient with improvised weapons, their damage increases to 1d6, or 1d8 when wielded in two hands, and your fists are considered improvised weapons.
- You can make an attack with an improvised weapon as a bonus action against a creature you are grappling.
- Whenever you roll damage for an unarmed strike or improvised weapon attack, you can reroll any die that roll a one, you must use the new roll.

### Tough:
Your hit point maximum increases by an amount equal to twice your level when you gain this feat. Whenever you gain a level thereafter, your hit point maximum increases by an additional 2 hit points.
