Your connection to the natural world takes the form of a draconic spirit, which can manifest in physical form as a drake. As your powers grow, your drake grows as well, blossoming from a small four-legged companion to a majestic winged creature large and strong enough for you to ride. Along the way, you gain an increasing share of the awe-inspiring power of dragons. *[[Ranger]] Subclass*

Consider the source of the draconic spirit you have bonded with. The Drakewarden Origin table offers examples.

| d6  | Draconic Bond                                                                                                                                                            |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | You studied a dragon’s scale or claw, or a trinket from a dragon’s hoard, creating your bond through that token’s lingering draconic magic.                              |
| 2   | A secret order of rangers who collect and guard draconic lore taught you their ways.                                                                                     |
| 3   | A dragon gave you a geode or gemstone to care for. To your surprise, the drake hatched from that stone.                                                                  |
| 4   | You ingested a few drops of dragon blood, forever infusing your nature magic with draconic power.                                                                        |
| 5   | An ancient Draconic inscription on a standing stone empowered you when you read it aloud.                                                                                |
| 6   | You had a vivid dream of a mysterious figure accompanied by seven yellow canaries, who warned you of impending doom. When you awoke, your drake was there, watching you. |

## Drake Warden Features:
### Draconic Gift  
*3rd-Level Drakewarden Feature*  
The bond you share with your drake creates a connection to dragonkind, granting you understanding and empowering your presence. You gain the following benefits:
- **Thaumaturgy.** You learn the *thaumaturgy* cantrip, which is a ranger spell for you.
- **Tongue of Dragons.** You learn to speak, read, and write Draconic or one other language of your choice.

### Drake Companion  
*3rd-Level Drakewarden Feature*  
As an action, you can magically summon the drake that is bound to you. It appears in an unoccupied space of your choice within 30 feet of you.  
The drake is friendly to you and your companions, and it obeys your commands. See its game statistics at the end of this document, which uses your proficiency bonus (PB) in several places. When you gain this feature, choose a damage type listed in its Draconic Essence trait. You can change the selected damage type for your dragon's draconic essence over the course of 10 days if you are not interrupted by combat or other adventuring activities. You can determine the cosmetic characteristics of the drake, such as its color, its scale texture, or any visible effect of its Draconic Essence; your choice has no effect on its game statistics.  
In combat, the drake acts during your turn. It can move and use its reaction on its own, but the only action it takes is the Dodge action, unless you use a bonus action to command it to take an action from its stat block, or another action. If you are incapacitated, the drake can take any action of its choice, not just Dodge.  
When your Companion is reduced to 0 hit points, it makes death saving throws like a player character would. If it dies, you can perform a special 1-hour ritual, which can be during a short or long rest, that restores it to life with 1 hit point. As part of this ritual, it can expend hit dice to regain hit points.

### Bond of Fang and Scale  
*7th-Level Drakewarden Feature*  
The bond you share with your drake intensifies, protecting you and stoking the drake’s fury. When you summon your drake, it grows wings on its back and gains a flying speed equal to its walking speed. In addition, you and the drake gain the following benefits:
- **Drake Mount.** The drake grows to Medium size. Reflecting your special bond, you can use the drake as a mount if your size is Medium or smaller. While you are riding your drake, it can’t use its flying speed.
- **Magic Fang.** The drake’s Bite attack deals an extra 1d6 damage of the type chosen for the drake’s Draconic Essence.
- **Resistance.** You gain resistance to the damage type chosen for the drake’s Draconic Essence.
- **Infused Strikes.** While you have your drake companion summoned, once per turn when you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can choose to deal additional damage of your drake's draconic essence type equal to one roll of your [[Ranger#Favored Foe|favored foe]] die.

### Drake’s Breath  
*11th-Level Drakewarden Feature*  
As an action, you can command your drake to exhale a 30-foot cone of damaging breath. Each creature in the cone must make a Dexterity saving throw against your spell save DC, taking 8d6 damage of your dragon's draconic essence trait on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. This size of the cone increases to 60 feet and the damage increases to 10d6 when you reach 15th level in this class.  
Once you use this feature, you can’t do so again until you finish a long rest, unless you expend a spell slot of 3rd level or higher to use it again.
