Bounty Hunters protect humanoids from their own. Trained to hunt in cities and towns, they can track their prey through the dark alleyways and underbellies of any town or city. Trained to use various martial techniques, these rangers can confidently confront and subdue any dangerous humanoid. *[[Ranger]] Subclass*

---

## Bounty Hunter Features:

### Martial Superiority: 
*3rd-level Bounty Hunter Feature*
You have studied various maneuvers to better counter humanoid foes. At 3rd level, you gain the following features:

#### Superiority Dice:
You have two Superiority Dice, which are d4s for you. You must expend a Superiority Die to use a Maneuver. You regain all expended Dice when you finish a short or long rest. At 7th level, your superiority die become d6s, and at 15th level they become d8s.

#### Maneuvers Known:
You learn two Maneuvers of your choice from this list at the end of this Archetype description. You can use only one Maneuver per attack. When you gain a ranger level, you can replace one of your Maneuvers with another of your choice.

#### Saving Throws:
If a Maneuver requires a creature to make a saving throw, your Maneuver saving throw DC is calculated as follows:
**Maneuver save DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength or Dexterity modifier (your choice)**

### Ear to the Ground: 
*3rd-level Bounty Hunter Feature*
Once you spend a long rest in a settlement, city, or town you have advantage on any ability checks you make to gather information on contacts or factions, and to navigate the environments of that settlement.
You have also learned the language of the criminal world. You can identify, read, and communicate in Thieves' Cant.

### Dead or Alive: 
*7th-level Bounty Hunter Feature* 
Moving with a grappled creature has no penalty on your movement speed. Additionally, being within 5 feet of a hostile creature doesn’t impose disadvantage on your ranged attack rolls with nets.

### Improved Maneuvers: 
*7th & 15th-level Bounty Hunter Feature*
Your skills improve. Upon reaching 7th level, you gain one additional Superiority Die, and you learn two additional Maneuvers of your choice.
At 15th level, these skills increase further. You gain another Superiority Die (for a total of four), and you learn one more Maneuver of your choice.

### Unwavering: 
*11th-level Bounty Hunter Feature*
You are a master of disabling foes. When your Quarry misses you with a melee attack, you can force it to make a Dexterity saving throw against your maneuver Save DC as a reaction. On a failure, it suffers one of the following effects of your choice:
- You can knock it prone and reduce its movement speed to 0 if it is Large or smaller.
- You can automatically grapple it if you have a free hand.

### The Most Dangerous Game: 
*15th-level Bounty Hunter Feature*
Your supernatural instincts allow you to predict your foe's attacks. Any time you take damage from your [[Ranger#Favored Foe|favored foe]], the damage is reduced by an amount equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1).

### Bounty Hunter Maneuvers:

|--------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Blinding Shot**         | As a bonus action, you can expend a Superiority Die and force a creature within 5 feet to make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, it takes piercing damage equal to your Superiority Die + your PB and is blinded until the start of your next turn. |
| **Brace Up**              | As a bonus action on your turn, you can expend a Superiority Die to grant yourself temporary hit points equal to your Superiority Die + your Constitution modifier (minimum of 1).                                                             |
| **Dirty Hit**             | When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can expend a Superiority Die and force it to make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, it takes additional damage equal to your Superiority Die + your PB, and until the start of your next turn, it cannot take reactions and its movement speed is halved. |
| **Disarm**                | When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can expend a Superiority Die to force them to make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes additional damage equal to your Superiority Die + your PB, and it drops one item that it is currently holding (your choice) at its feet. |
| **Disorienting Blow**     | When you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack, you can expend a Superiority Die to force them to make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes additional damage equal to your Superiority Die + your PB, and on its next turn, its speed is halved, and it can use either an action or a bonus action. It can’t make more than one attack during that turn. |
| **First Aid**             | As an action, you can expend a Superiority Die and touch a conscious and willing creature. The creature gains temporary hit points equal to one roll of your Superiority Die + your PB. Additionally, as a reaction, the target can expend one of their Hit Dice, regaining hit points from it as normal. |
| **Lightstep**             | Whenever you make a Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) or Dexterity (Stealth) check, you can expend a Superiority Die and add it to your roll. You can use this Exploit after you roll, but before you know if you succeed or fail. |
| **Naturalist's Intuition**| When you make a Wisdom (Animal Handling) or Intelligence (Nature) check, you can expend a Superiority Die and add it to your roll. You can use this Maneuver after you roll, but before you know whether you succeed or fail. |
| **Riposte**               | When you are hit by a melee attack, you can use your reaction to expend a Superiority Die, adding it to your Armor Class against that attack. If the attack misses, you can immediately make one weapon attack against the attacker. |
| **Survivalist's Cunning** | When you make a Wisdom (Medicine) or Wisdom (Survival) check, you can expend a Superiority Die and add it to your roll. You can use this Maneuver after you roll, but before you know whether you succeed or fail. |
| **Sweeping Strike**       | When you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack, you can expend a Superiority Die to force it to make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, it falls prone and takes bludgeoning damage equal to your Superiority Die + your PB. Creatures more than one size larger than you have advantage on this saving throw. |
| **Take Down**             | As a bonus action, you can expend a Superiority Die to make a Shove or Grapple attack against a creature within your reach, adding your Superiority Die to your Strength (Athletics) roll. |
