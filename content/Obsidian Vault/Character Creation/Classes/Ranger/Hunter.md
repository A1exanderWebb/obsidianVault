Rangers are as varied as the lands that they hail from. Some use their knowledge of the wilderness to become guides and naturalists. Others use their skills to become trackers or traders. Some wander the countryside, while others stand as guardians of sacred forests. While they fulfill various roles, deep down inside every ranger beats the heart of a Hunter. Marauding hordes of Orcs, vile trolls, great and terrible dragons, or great beasts of the wild, it matters not to a true Hunter. They always find a way to overcome their quarry. *[[Ranger]] Subclass*

## Hunter Features:
### Hunter's Prey:  
*3rd-level Hunter feature*  
You have specialized your fighting style to counter certain monsters. At 3rd level, you gain one of the following features:
- **Colossus Slayer**. When you hit your [[Ranger#Favored Foe|favored foe]] with a weapon attack and it has less than half its maximum hit points, you roll one additional die for your [[Ranger#Favored Foe|favored foe]] damage.
- **Crippling Strike**. Once per turn when you hit your [[Ranger#Favored Foe|favored foe]] with a weapon attack, you can force it to make a Constitution saving throw against your ranger save DC. On a failed save, its speed is reduced to 0 until the start of your next turn.
- **Giant Killer**. When your [[Ranger#Favored Foe|favored foe]] misses you with an attack, you can make one attack against that creature as a reaction.
- **Horde Breaker**. Once per turn when you make a weapon attack against a your [[Ranger#Favored Foe|favored foe]], you can make one additional attack with the same weapon against another creature within 10 feet of your original target and within the range of your weapon.

### Defensive Tactics:  
*7th-level Hunter feature*  
You have honed your skills to better protect yourself from your foes. At 7th level, you gain one of the following features:
- **Escape the Horde**. When a creature targets you with an opportunity attack, they have disadvantage on their attack roll so long as you can see the attacking creature. You also have advantage to escape or resist any effect that would grapple or restrain you.
- **Steel Will**. You have advantage on saving throws to resist the charmed, frightened, and stunned conditions.
- **Multiattack Defense**. When a creature hits you with an attack, you gain a bonus to your Armor Class equal to your proficiency bonus against all attacks made by that creature for the rest of the turn.
- **Sturdy Frame**. When you take non-magical bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage that damage is reduced 2.

### Multiattack:  
*11th-level Hunter feature*  
You have mastered specialized techniques to thwart your foes. At 11th level, you gain one of the following features:
- **Rapid Strike**. If you take the Attack action on your turn and have advantage on an attack roll against one of the targets, you can forgo the advantage for that roll to make one additional weapon attack against that target, as part of the same action. You can do so no more than once per turn.
- **Volley**. As an action, you can make a ranged attack against any number of creatures within 10 feet of a point within your weapon's range. You roll a separate attack roll for each target, and you must have ammunition for each individual attack.
- **Whirlwind Attack**. As an action, you can move up to half of your movement speed, as part of this movement you can make a melee attack against any number of creatures that you pass within 5 ft of. Make a separate attack roll for each target.

### Superior Hunter's Defense:  
*15th-level Hunter feature*  
You have mastered defending yourself from wild and vicious prey. At 15th level, you gain one of the following features:
- **Evasion**. When you are subjected to an effect that allows you to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, you instead take no damage if you succeed on the saving throw, and only half damage if you fail.
- **Stand Against the Tide**. On each of its turns, your [[Ranger#Favored Foe| Favored Foe]] has disadvantage on all attacks it makes against you until it hits you with an attack.
- **Uncanny Dodge**. When a creature you can see hits you with an attack, you can use your reaction to halve the damage you would take from that attack.
