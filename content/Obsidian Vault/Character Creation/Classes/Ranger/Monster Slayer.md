Every culture tells stories of evil spirits and monsters that live just beyond the walls of civilization. Thanks to the quiet watch of dedicated rangers, these terrible monsters remain nothing more than a story. These guardian rangers take up the mantle of Monster Slayer, vowing to protect the innocent from the things of the night. They seek out vampires, evil fey, werewolves, and other such vile creatures and destroy them. *[[Ranger]] Subclass*

---

## Monster Slayer Magic:

You learn the following spells at the ranger levels noted in the table below. They count as ranger spells for you, you always have them prepared, and they don't count against the total number of ranger spells you prepare each day.

|                  |                                           |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| **Ranger Level** | **Spell**                                 |
| 3rd              | *compelled duel, protection from evil/good* |
| 5th              | *see invisibility, moonbeam*                |
| 9th              | *magic circle, protection from energy*      |
| 13th             | *banishment, faithful hound*                |
| 17th             | *dispel evil and good, hold monster*        |

## Monster Slayer Features:

### Slayer's Mark:  
*3rd-level Monster Slayer feature*  
You have a keen eye for the weak points of your foes. You regain one expended use of your [[Ranger#Favored Foe| Favored Foe]] feature whenever you complete a short rest. Moreover, while you have a creature marked as your [[Ranger#Favored Foe|favored foe]] you can use your bonus action to learn one of the following characteristics of your choice:
- Armor Class
- Any Damage Vulnerabilities
- Saving Throw Proficiency
- Any Damage Immunities
- Skill Proficiency
- Any Damage Resistances
- Spellcasting Ability  

### Iron Focus:  
*7th-level Monster Slayer feature*  
Your resolute focus allows you to better resist the creatures you hunt. Beginning at 7th level, whenever your [[Ranger#Favored Foe| Favored Foe]] forces you to make a saving throw, you gain a bonus equal to one roll of your favored foe die.

### Monster Slayer:  
*11th-level Monster Slayer feature*  
Your attacks and spells against your [[Ranger#Favored Foe| Favored Foe]] treat its damage immunities as resistances, and ignore any damage resistances it has.  

### Supernatural Reflexes:  
*11th-level Monster Slayer feature*  
When your [[Ranger#Favored Foe| Favored Foe]] misses you with an attack or you succeed on a saving throw it forced you to make, you can use your reaction to make a single weapon attack against it.

### Slayer’s Counter:  
*15th-level Monster Slayer feature*  
Starting at 15th level, you can counter the attempts of your foes to harm you or your allies. As a reaction when your [[Ranger#Favored Foe| Favored Foe]] forces you or an ally you can see within 30 feet of you to make a saving throw, you can make one weapon attack targeting them before you or your ally make the saving throw. This weapon attack can be made with a weapon you are currently wielding or a thrown weapon you are carrying on your person. On hit, you or the targeted ally automatically succeed on the saving throw. 
Once you use this reaction, you must finish a short or long rest before you can use it again. If you have no uses left, you can expend a spell slot of 1st-level or higher to use it again.
